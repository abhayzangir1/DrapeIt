# Demo Script — 2:45 Target

Record on a real target phone. Keep the final cut between 1 and 3 minutes; judges are not required to watch beyond 3 minutes. Show the same tested APK that the repository instructions build.

## Preflight before recording

- Complete the priority `DEVICE_TEST_MATRIX.md` path on both phones when possible; at minimum, complete it on the exact phone used in the demo and state the second phone's honest status.
- Deploy the Worker and confirm `/healthz`, session creation, both paid tasks, and exact unit costs.
- Confirm the dashboard balance matches the configured ledger baseline and leaves at least 300 units after planned demo tasks.
- Use a consenting adult, broad indirect light, a clean lens, and one solid matte fabric.
- Disable beauty filters and notifications; turn on Do Not Disturb.
- Clear unrelated records and use a fresh judge access code that can remain active through August 31, 2026.
- Record a clean screen capture plus a second camera angle showing the real fabric and phone together. Do not expose the API key, access code, task URLs, private face files, or device serial.
- Use only owned/licensed visuals, music, and product references. YouTube is preferred by Devpost; upload publicly.

## Shot list and narration

### 0:00–0:15 — The gap

**Picture:** Person holds one fabric beside the face; cut to DrapeProof home.

**Say:**

> Online color chips cannot show what a real cloth does beside your face, and a phone camera quietly changes exposure when the cloth enters. DrapeProof turns that uncertain moment into evidence—without telling anyone what is beautiful.

### 0:15–0:35 — Trust contract

**Picture:** Home screen; briefly highlight “No app beauty filter,” the three signals, and refusal card.

**Say:**

> It reports cloth–skin separation, feature definition, and only when controls pass, an apparent camera-recorded face shift. No season, beauty score, or medical inference.

### 0:35–1:15 — Real-cloth controlled scan

**Picture:** Tap **Start a real-cloth scan**. Show opening baseline, add the same physical matte fabric into the dashed zone, drape readings, remove it, and closing baseline. Keep a side-angle inset proving the cloth is real.

**Say:**

> DrapeProof dims the screen, samples multiple frames on-device with MediaPipe without applying its own beauty or color transform, then locks exposure and white balance when the phone supports it. The phone camera pipeline may still perform its normal ISP processing. DrapeProof closes with a second baseline so lighting drift cannot masquerade as a skin response.

Do not hide a failed gate. If the first scan downgrades, briefly explain the failure, correct the setup, and retake—or use that honest downgrade as the next shot.

### 1:15–1:38 — Evidence, not verdict

**Picture:** Result screen. Point to tier, `ΔE00`, signed `ΔL*`, feature change, and apparent shift/withheld state. Save it.

**Say:**

> This controlled pair passed our pose, clipping, stability, focus, control, and baseline checks. Separation is a difference, not a score. Apparent shift describes only what this locked camera recorded; it never claims intrinsic skin changed.

If the tier is not controlled pair, replace “passed” with the actual tier and say why the third signal was withheld.

### 1:38–1:58 — Purchase decision

**Picture:** Catalog. Tap Soft, Balanced, Bold. Show the recommendation changing and save one Drape Record.

**Say:**

> The shopper chooses the contrast they want. DrapeProof ranks only the six variants of this exact demo SKU by within-SKU percentile. It never turns “bold” into “better,” and it carries the capture's evidence tier into the decision.

Show the limitation that current demo colors are screen hex specifications, not measured retailer swatches.

### 1:58–2:30 — YouCam integration

**Picture:** Open **YouCam Lab** from Home. Show health/credit reserve, consent, a Facial Color Tones result, then Clothes V3 using a front-facing person image and an owned apparel reference. Waiting can be time-compressed, but show the accepted task and real returned result.

**Say:**

> With explicit consent, a secure Worker keeps the YouCam key off the phone. Facial Color Tones gives a secondary palette, and Clothes V3 visualizes an apparel reference on the person. Paid operations are cost-checked and atomically preserve a 300-unit floor against the dashboard baseline configured for this demo. Generated pixels never change the physical contrast rank.

Do not use a prerecorded fake or static mock as if it were an API result. The deployed and paid-tested provider is Clothes V3; do not describe the optional, unprovisioned Scarf/R2 path as the live demo.

### 2:30–2:45 — Close

**Picture:** Drape Record with metrics/limitations; end card with repo URL.

**Say:**

> DrapeProof gives shoppers a repeatable way to compare real cloth, photos, and exact product colors—while preserving uncertainty. Color evidence, not color rules.
